Mosquito detector

Readings depend on this device’s microphone and hardware. Not calibrated; for orientation only.

Listens for a steady, narrow tone at ~300–900 Hz (wingbeat) that holds for about a second, stands well above the room noise, and has 2x / 3x harmonics. Honest limits: it only works when the mosquito is very close to the mic and the room is quiet. It will not detect a distant mosquito, and whistles, beeps, fans and electronics can trigger it.

Approaching aircraft detector

Readings depend on this device’s microphone and hardware. Not calibrated; for orientation only.

Sound-pattern spotting, not reliable identification. Wind, traffic, HVAC and fans cause false positives, and range is limited to what the phone mic can hear. Helicopter = 10–40 Hz rotor “thump”; drone = 100–400 Hz harmonic series that wobbles; airplane = broadband rumble (prop planes add a tonal blade-pass). Approaching / receding comes from the level trend over several seconds (plus Doppler pitch drift for tonal sounds, a weak cue).
